Like a real interview, the questions are of different kinds. The kind of each question is already fixed, in this order:
{slots}

What each kind needs:
- resume: something specific from the candidate's resume that matters for the role: a project, a past job, a technology they used. Name it, for example "Payments API built with FastAPI".
- concept: a core concept that anyone in this role must know, chosen from the role and the job description (if one is given), even if the resume does not mention it. Name the general topic, not a resume item. For example "Database indexing" or "REST vs GraphQL".
- scenario: a realistic on-the-job situation for this role, named by its problem, for example "Slow API under heavy traffic" or "Production bug found after release".
- behavioural: an HR or soft-skill topic, for example "Handling conflict in a team" or "Working under a tight deadline".

Guidelines:
- Fit the depth to the level and years of experience.
- Cover different areas of the role. Do not let the resume decide every topic: the concept and scenario questions should test what the role needs, including areas the resume does not show.
- No duplicates or near-duplicates.
